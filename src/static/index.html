How I work

From brief to published

01

Brief

You send a brief, or we build one together if you don't have one yet. Keywords, audience, angle, and word count agreed before anything gets written.

02

Draft

First draft written against that brief, with structure, keywords, and tone locked in from the start rather than patched in afterwards.

03

Audit

I read it back against the brief and fact check anything that needs it, before it goes anywhere near you or your reviewers.

04

Deliver

Delivered on the agreed deadline, formatted and ready to publish, with revisions handled inside the same thread.

What lands in your inbox

Standard with every piece
  • Publish-ready draft in Google Docs, formatted with your heading hierarchy
  • Meta title and description written to the character limit, not truncated
  • Internal link suggestions mapped to pages you already have
  • Sources and fact-check notes so reviewers can verify claims fast
  • Inline comments resolved before the doc comes back to you
